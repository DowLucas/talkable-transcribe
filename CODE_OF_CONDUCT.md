# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior includes:**
- Being respectful and considerate
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what's best for the community
- Showing empathy towards others

**Unacceptable behavior includes:**
- Harassment, discrimination, or trolling
- Publishing others' private information
- Any conduct inappropriate for a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly.
